Line of Legends Support

Stuck, found a bug, or a purchase didn't arrive? Email lineoflegends.help@gmail.com. Tell us your hero's name, level and class, and what happened. We usually answer within two days.

A purchase didn't arrive

Open the Store in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Bundles and the Ink Pass come back right away. If gems are still missing, email us with the date of the purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Cancel the Ink Pass

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Line of Legends.
