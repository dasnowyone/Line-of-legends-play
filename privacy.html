Line of Legends Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Line of Legends is made by SnowyOne. This policy explains what the game collects and why.

What we collect

The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. To run the game we collect:

  • Your game save. It is kept on your device, with a backup copy in your iPhone's secure keychain, so you keep your heroes and purchases if you reinstall.
  • Purchase records. Purchases are made through Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm them. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID and your purchase history, not your name or payment details.
  • Anonymous gameplay stats, such as levels reached, dungeons cleared and store visits. We use these to improve the game. They are tied to a random ID, not to you.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show targeted ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the save from your device. To delete purchase and stats records tied to your random ID, or the keychain backup, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
